Student loan refinance calculator

What to check before you refinance

Federal loans need extra care. If you refinance a federal loan with a private lender, you generally give up federal protections, such as income-driven repayment options and forgiveness programs. Once you refinance, you can't go back.

Also check for fees, whether the rate is fixed or variable, and whether you need a co-signer. This calculator doesn't include fees.

A longer term costs more

Stretching the loan over more years lowers your monthly payment, but you usually pay more interest in total. Try changing the new term and watch the savings shrink.

Common questions

Who can get a lower rate?

Lenders usually offer their best rates to people with a good credit history and steady income. Your offer may be higher than what you see advertised, so use the real offer you receive.

Should I refinance private or federal loans?

Refinancing private loans mainly comes down to the numbers. With federal loans, you also lose federal benefits, so think carefully and check studentaid.gov first.

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Stipendex offers free calculators for students. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use the site.

The short version

  • The numbers you type into our calculators stay in your browser. We don't receive them, store them or sell them.
  • We don't ask you to sign up or give us your name or email.
  • The site may show ads from other companies, which can use cookies.

What we collect

Stipendex does not intentionally collect or store the numbers you enter into its calculators. The calculations run in your browser, so those inputs are not sent to Stipendex as part of the calculator itself. Other services used by the site, such as hosting, analytics or advertising, may process information as described below.

Hosting

Stipendex is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when you visit. You can read how they handle this in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google may receive your IP address and basic browser details.

Analytics

Stipendex uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage, such as which pages and calculator tools are used. The analytics code does not send the numbers you enter into calculators as event data. You can learn more about Google Analytics and its privacy controls in Google’s documentation.

Advertising and cookies

We don't set our own cookies. Stipendex may show ads from third-party networks such as Google AdSense. These partners can use cookies or similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalized ads in Google's ad settings or at aboutads.info.

Links to other sites

If we link to other websites, we don't control them and they have their own privacy policies.

Children

Stipendex is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

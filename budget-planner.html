What is the 50/30/20 rule?

A simple guide: about 50% of your income for needs (rent, food, transport, bills), 30% for wants (fun, eating out), and 20% for saving or paying off debt.

Example: on $1,000 a month, that's about $500 for needs, $300 for wants and $200 to save.

Student reality check

Students often spend more than half their income on rent, so the exact split may not fit you. Use it as a rough guide, not a rule.

Common questions

What counts as a need?

Things you can't easily skip: housing, food, transport, phone, books and loan payments. Fun and everything else counts as a want here.

What if I'm spending more than I earn?

The planner shows how much you're over by. Look at the biggest category first, since small cuts there make the biggest difference.

How UK student loan repayments work

You only repay when you earn above a threshold that depends on your plan. Then you repay 9% of everything above it, or 6% for a postgraduate loan. The money is taken from your pay automatically.

Example: Plan 2, £35,000 salary. £35,000 − £29,385 = £5,615. 9% of that is £505.35 a year, or about £42.11 a month.

Your monthly repayment depends on your salary, not on how much you owe. Interest affects how long it takes, not what is taken each month.

Common questions

Which plan am I on?

It depends on where and when you started your course. Log in to your student loan account or check gov.uk to see your plan. If you're unsure, don't guess.

Can I have two loans at once?

Yes. If you also have a postgraduate loan, you repay it at the same time as your main loan. Tick the box above to include it.

Is this exact?

It's an estimate. Real deductions are worked out each pay period, so your payslip may differ slightly. Bonuses and overtime can change it too.

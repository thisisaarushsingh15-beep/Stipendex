CGPA to percentage calculator (10-point scale)

How the conversion works

There is no single official formula. The most common one multiplies your CGPA by 9.5, which CBSE uses. Some universities multiply by 10, and some subtract a small number first and then multiply.

Example: a CGPA of 8.0 with the CBSE method is 8.0 × 9.5 = 76%.

For a custom formula, the calculator does (CGPA − the number you subtract) × the number you multiply by.

Use your university's rule

Universities publish their own formulas, and different websites often disagree about them. That's why this tool lets you type in your own. Check your university's official circular, especially for jobs or applications abroad.

Common questions

Which formula should I use?

Use the one your university or the organisation asking for it accepts. If nothing is specified, the CBSE method (× 9.5) is available as a reference here, but it is not a universal rule.

Does this work for a 4-point scale?

No. This tool is for 10-point CGPA systems. For a 4.0 scale, see our GPA calculator.

How the savings goal works

Take your goal, subtract what you already have, and split the rest across the months you have left. If your savings earn interest, you need to put in a bit less each month.

Example: a $1,200 goal, $0 saved, 12 months, no interest. $1,200 ÷ 12 = $100 a month.

Quick tips

Set up an automatic transfer on payday so you save before you spend. Keep the money in a separate savings account so it's harder to touch.

Common questions

Do I need to include interest?

No. Set the interest to 0 and it's plain division. Many student savings accounts pay a small rate, so 0 is a safe, cautious choice.

What if I can't save enough each month?

Fill in the box for what you can save. The planner shows how many months it will take, so you can move your date or lower your goal.

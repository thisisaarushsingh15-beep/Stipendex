UK degree classification calculator

How degree classes work

Most UK universities turn your marks into a weighted average, then match it to a class. Years that count more, usually the final year, pull the average harder.

Common boundaries: First 70% and above, 2:1 60% to 69%, 2:2 50% to 59%, Third 40% to 49%.

The starting weights above (Year 1 counts for 0%, Year 2 for 33%, final year for 67%) are common, but change them to match your university.

What this can't know

Universities differ. Some use the best marks from your credits, some have borderline rules that lift students just under a boundary, and some count year 1. Your course handbook has the official rules, so this is an estimate only.

Common questions

Does first year count?

At many universities it doesn't, but at some it counts for a small share. Check your handbook and set the first-year weight to match.

What if I'm just below a boundary?

Many universities look again at students a point or two below a boundary, for example if most of their credits are in the higher class. Ask your department how it works.
